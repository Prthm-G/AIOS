# Programme and ad evidence matrix

**Task ID:** `P0-EVIDENCE-2026-07-28`

**Observed on:** 2026-07-28 (Asia/Kolkata)

**Scope:** Eight high-intent Online programmes from Lovely Professional University, Desh Bhagat University, and Amity University, Uttar Pradesh

**Status:** Independently reviewed; no record approved for ledger promotion or advertising

**Change class:** R1 — new internal evidence document only

## Decision summary

**None of the eight records is cleared for advertising.** The current UGC-DEB programme-list page is labelled beta and does not provide a dependable, complete 2026-27 result set for these programmes. It currently exposes an Online (Entitled) row whose Year field is 2026-27 for Amity University and Bachelor of Computer Applications, but its rendered Session and State fields are blank. The same result set also includes a test record and omits selected programmes that appear for 2025-26. Absence from this beta result set is therefore treated as unresolved, not as evidence that a programme is unauthorized.

Amity Online BCA has the strongest currently surfaced UGC-table evidence in this selected set. That narrow observation does not prove it is closest overall or first to clear. It remains blocked because the beta table's Year field shows 2026-27 but its rendered Session field is blank, the official page does not explicitly map its July 2026 admissions window to that regulator year/session, its eligibility statements conflict, and its fee basis is not release-ready.

The UGC-DEB home page gives **15 September 2026** as the last date for HEIs to take admissions for the academic session beginning August 2026, with admission data due 18 September 2026. This creates a material planning constraint: the 30 September business target must not assume that new admissions can be taken after the regulator's published deadline.

## Evidence classes

- **Regulator primary:** a current UGC-DEB page, row, notice, or publication. Release-grade regulator evidence must resolve the exact awarding HEI, programme, mode, and academic session; an observation with blank fields does not meet that standard.
- **University primary:** the university's current statement about its own programme, eligibility, duration, fee, or intake. This does not independently prove regulatory entitlement.
- **Internal record:** Skeure's ledger, website content, brochure copy, or prior human note. This is useful for drift detection but is not primary evidence.
- **Inference:** a conservative conclusion drawn from the sources above. Inference is not restated as a sourced fact.
- **Platform estimate:** none used in this research.

## Release gate used

A programme can move to an ad build only when all of these are true:

1. The awarding HEI, exact programme name, Online mode, and current academic session match a current regulator record.
2. The university's current official page names or otherwise unambiguously maps to the advertised intake.
3. Eligibility and every fee used in copy are internally consistent, dated, and scoped to the same programme and intake.
4. The ad, landing page, and counselling workflow can use the same verified facts.
5. High-risk recognition, ranking, placement, salary, recruiter, urgency, testimonial, and finance claims are excluded unless separately evidenced.
6. A reviewer other than the researcher approves the record.

## Programme records

### `EVD-LPU-OL-MBA-20260728` — LPU Online MBA

**Regulator primary**

- The UGC-DEB programme table showed Lovely Professional University, Punjab, Master of Business Administration, Online, in the 2025-26 results observed on 2026-07-28.
- No exact 2026-27 LPU Online MBA row was observed in that result set. Because the table is labelled beta and contains incomplete/test data, this is an unresolved current-session gap, not an adverse entitlement finding.

**University primary**

- The current official page describes a two-year/four-semester Online MBA.
- The page's live data gave an application deadline of 30 July 2026.
- A first-pass inspection observed ₹40,400 per semester. The independent reviewer could not reproduce that tuition amount from the currently delivered public text, so a dated capture or official programme guide is required before the amount can be used.
- The current public page does reproduce a ₹2,000 examination fee per semester and a non-refundable ₹1,000 registration fee treatment.
- Eligibility is inconsistent on the same official page: one section says graduation in any discipline or equivalent, while another specifies at least 50%, or 45% for a reserved category, with no entrance examination.

**Internal record and drift**

- `programme-ledger.csv` currently marks this record verified following a named human check on 2026-07-25, but its `academic_session` remains `unconfirmed`.
- The observed live sources support retaining the programme as a candidate, but not clearing a 2026-27 ad. No ledger status was changed in this task.

**Inference:** Block advertising until an exact 2026-27 UGC-DEB record or equivalent current regulator document is captured and the eligibility contradiction is resolved in writing. This conclusion applies only to **LPU Online MBA**; LPU Distance/ODL remains outside the advertising scope and disabled by programme instruction.

## Claims explicitly excluded from ad clearance

- rankings, accreditation beyond the exact programme entitlement, international equivalence, or foreign acceptance;
- placement rates, placement assistance outcomes, salary figures, recruiter logos, hiring-partner counts, or job guarantees;
- limited-seat, scarcity, deadline, or urgency claims other than a current dated source used for the same intake;
