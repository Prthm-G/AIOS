## Phase 5 · Consolidate the Meta estate

Not urgent, genuinely important. Skeure's advertising currently runs across **three Facebook
identities, two ad accounts, two WABAs and two phone numbers**, and the account generating all the
live leads is a personal one.

### What is already fine

**Kuanli needs no work.** Both numbers are registered under the same account
(`16cb2ac1-b074-4996-9a2e-260c123eabfe`) in `whatsapp_config`, so multi-number is already solved on
the CRM side and both feed one inbox, one contact list, one pipeline.

| Number | phone_number_id | WABA | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| +91 86996 00020 | 110485318348695 | 106777392057661 | LPU Distance ads land here |
| 919592200021 | 1197620893430601 | 2170658457111515 | the number on the website |

### What is not fine

| Asset | Sits under | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Ad account `act_278258370` | a personal Facebook profile, no business | **Single point of failure.** Three years and ₹4 lakh of history, currently the only live lead source. If that profile is restricted or lost, the account goes with it and there is no recovery path. |
| Ad account `act_961766249917785` | business `1593889128670416` | fine, but capped and idle |
| WABA `106777392057661` | unconfirmed | carries the live ad traffic |
| Three separate identities | | no single admin view, no clean succession, permissions granted person to person |

### Sequencing, and the rule that governs it

**Never move the asset that is currently carrying the leads.** Consolidation is worth doing and worth
doing slowly.

1. **Now:** nothing. Phase 1 has been capturing for hours. Let it run and build a baseline.
2. **After CAPI (1.4) is live and proven:** confirm which business owns WABA `106777392057661`, and
   whether its dataset is separate from the one on the DegreeCraft account.
3. **Then:** claim `act_278258370` into business `1593889128670416`. One-way, and it moves billing,
   so it happens once and deliberately. This is what removes the single point of failure.
4. **Then:** bring the WABA and both phone numbers under the same business portfolio.
5. **Last:** retire whichever ad account is not being used, rather than leaving two live.

### The design constraint this puts on Phase 1.4

Two WABAs means potentially **two Conversions API datasets**. A conversion has to be reported to the
dataset belonging to the WABA the conversation actually arrived on, not to a single hardcoded one.
`conversations` can be joined to `whatsapp_config` to determine that per conversation. Build the CAPI
sender to look it up rather than assume, or attribution will silently post to the wrong dataset the
first time a lead comes in on the other number.
