# Google Business Profile · setup runbook

For "education consultant Patiala", "LPU admission Patiala" and similar local searches, the Google
Map pack sits ABOVE every organic result. Your physical Patiala office is an advantage no national
online-education competitor has. This is the single highest-leverage local SEO action available.

**Status: not started as of 2026-08-22.** Postcard verification is the long pole (5-14 days), so
start step 4 first and fill everything else while waiting.

---

## The one rule: use the right Google account

Sign in with **the same Google account that now owns the `education.skeure.com` Search Console
property** (the new one, after the 2026-08-22 owner cleanup). Keeping one identity across Search
Console, the OAuth client, and GBP is what avoids the lost-account mess that cost a day. Do not
create this under a personal throwaway account.

---

## Steps

### 1. Go to the console
<https://business.google.com> → sign in with the Google account above.

### 2. Find or create
Search your business name.
- **If a listing already exists** (someone may have created one, or Google auto-generated it from
  Maps data): claim it via **"Request access"** or **"Claim this business"**. Do NOT create a
  duplicate — duplicates split reviews and can get both suppressed.
- **If nothing exists:** **Add your business**.

### 3. Categories
- **Primary:** `Education center`
- **Secondary:** `Educational consultant`, `Distance learning center`

Primary category is a major ranking factor — get it right. You can add secondaries later.

### 4. Address + verification (START THIS FIRST — it is the long pole)
Enter the real Patiala office address. This triggers **postcard verification**: Google mails a code
to that address, usually 5-14 days. Nothing goes live until you enter the code, so kick it off
before doing anything else.

- If offered phone or email verification instead, take it — instant.
- The address must be a real location you can receive mail at.

### 5. Fill everything while the postcard is in transit
- **Hours:** Mon-Sat, 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM (from the WhatsApp lead samples; adjust if wrong).
- **Phone:** the number students already reach you on.
- **Website:** `https://education.skeure.com`
- **Service area:** Patiala + surrounding Punjab districts.
- **Description:** short, plain, what you do — counsel students into UGC-recognised online/distance
  UG and PG programs from partner universities. No keyword stuffing.
- **Photos:** 8-10 of the actual office, exterior + interior + team. Photos materially affect both
  ranking and click-through. This is not optional polish.

### 6. Services
Add the universities you counsel for (LPU, Amity, Chandigarh, Punjabi University, MMU, etc.) and the
program types (Online MBA, Online MA, BCA, etc.) as Services. These mirror the new program pages on
the site, which reinforces relevance.

### 7. After verification
- Put the GBP link on WhatsApp and in your email signature.
- **Ask enrolled/happy students for reviews.** Review count and recency are the biggest Map-pack
  ranking levers, and you already have satisfied students. A steady trickle beats a one-time burst.
- Post an "Admissions open" **Update** each session; posts keep the profile active in Google's eyes.

---

## What this cannot be done for you

GBP needs your Google login, your real address to receive the postcard, and photos of your premises.
It is entirely an operator task — no part of it is scriptable or delegable.

## Why it beats backlinks for this business

A national online-education aggregator cannot rank in the Patiala Map pack — they have no Patiala
address. You do. For local-intent searches (the ones that convert to walk-ins and calls), a verified
GBP with reviews outperforms any backlink you could realistically acquire. Pair it with the
`/programs/*` pages already live and the local-intent funnel is covered end to end.
