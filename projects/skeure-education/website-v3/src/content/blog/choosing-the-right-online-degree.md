---
title: "How to Choose the Right Online Degree"
description: "A few questions worth asking before you commit to any online UG or PG program."
pubDate: 2026-07-01
category: "Guides"
---

Before comparing universities, get clear on three things: what job or next step this degree is actually for, how many hours a week you can realistically study, and whether you'd rather learn live or at your own pace.
