question: "Is an online or distance degree valid for government jobs and higher studies?"
answer: "Yes, when it is from a UGC-recognised university in an approved mode. Online and distance degrees that are UGC-DEB entitled are treated as equivalent to regular degrees, including for government jobs, further studies, and most private employers. The key is that the specific program is approved, which is exactly what we check for you. Every university we work with, and the modes we recommend, are ones we can confirm are recognised, and we will show you the recognition status before you enroll."
