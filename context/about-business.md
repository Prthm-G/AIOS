# About the Business

Education consulting institution operating out of Patiala, Punjab, partnered with multiple national universities across India (including LPU and Amity). Counsels students into online UG and PG degree programs — handling eligibility, fees, curriculum questions, and applications.

**ICP:** Prospective students (and their families) in and around Patiala/Punjab seeking accredited online undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.

**Revenue model:** Students pay tuition/fees directly to the universities. The business earns commission back from each university partner, paid into a separate bank account per university — currently untracked and unstreamlined. No standard accounting or tracking software in place yet.
