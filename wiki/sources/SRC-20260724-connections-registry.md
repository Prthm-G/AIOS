# Connections — AIOS System Registry

## Citation

`connections.md`, root of the AIOS repo. Generated by the `/onboard` skill from intake questions Q4–Q7. Copied into `raw/` as `2026-07-24-connections-registry.md` for wiki ingest.

## Summary

A 7-row registry mapping every domain of the business (revenue, customer interactions, calendar, communication, task tracking, meeting intelligence, knowledge/files) to its current tool, connection mechanism, and auth status. As of this ingest, all 7 domains are marked "not yet connected" — the registry documents intended or in-use tools, not live integrations.

## Key claims

- Revenue/financials has no tracking system; commissions land in separate bank accounts per university partner, untracked. (row 1)
- Customer interactions run through WhatsApp via a bot handler named **Auretris**, plus phone. (row 2)
- Calendar tool is unconfirmed — candidates are Gmail or Outlook calendar. (row 3)
- University-facing communication is email (docs exchange only); day-to-day is WhatsApp/phone. (row 4)
- No formal project/task tracking exists; **Kuanli**, a WhatsApp CRM, is intended to fill this role once live. (row 5)
- No meeting-recording tool is in place. (row 6)
- Files are split across three locations: brochures at `/home/user/automation_stack/brochures`, a university FAQ dataset in a PostgreSQL database, and other internal files on a local office server. (row 7)
- None of the 7 domains have a working connection mechanism yet — all read "not yet connected" as of 2026-07-24.

## Evidence and data

- Structured as a markdown table, one row per domain, columns: Domain, Tool, Mechanism, Auth, Last checked. All "Last checked" cells are empty (`—`), meaning no domain has been verified live since the registry was created.
- Mechanism taxonomy defined below the table: `mcp`, `script`, `export`, `key+ref`, `not yet connected` — this is the AIOS's own vocabulary for describing integration type, not evidence about the business itself.

## Entities and concepts

- [[wiki/entities/auretris|Auretris]] — the WhatsApp bot handler named in row 2.
- [[wiki/entities/kuanli|Kuanli]] — the WhatsApp CRM named in row 5, intended future home for task tracking.
- [[wiki/concepts/lead-management-automation|Lead management automation]] — the theme connecting rows 2 and 5 (customer interactions + task tracking), both currently manual and both slated for Auretris/Kuanli by end of July 2026 per `context/priorities.md`.

## Limitations and bias

- Self-reported intake data, not independently verified. No dates on when each domain's tool was last actually used or checked (all "Last checked" cells blank).
- The registry describes *intended* connection targets, not confirmed working integrations — treat "Tool" column entries as current-state facts, but "not yet connected" as the only confirmed mechanism status.
- Calendar tool is explicitly unresolved in the source itself (row 3 asks a question rather than stating a fact).
