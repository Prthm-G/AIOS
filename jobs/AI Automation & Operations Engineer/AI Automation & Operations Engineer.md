### About the Role

Aventyr Security Corporation is building the future of AI-managed operations. We’re hiring a full-time AI Automation & Operations Engineer to build intelligent workflows using tools like Crew.ai, and to automate real-world business processes across scheduling, finance, logistics, and compliance.

You'll work directly with the founder to prototype, deploy, and scale agent-based automations that replace manual tasks across the company. You’ll also learn core operational functions (like payroll and invoicing) specifically so you can automate them.

This is a role for someone smart, curious, and ruthlessly practical — someone who wants to build systems that run entire businesses, not just apps.

### What You'll Do

- Build agent workflows using **Crew.ai**, **Zapier**, **Odoo**, and custom logic
- Set up automations for:
	- **Guard scheduling & shift management**
		- **Accounts payable/receivable & payroll**
		- **Incident reporting, HR compliance, document workflows**
- Learn how business ops really work — and then automate them
- Build internal tools or interfaces (Airtable, Streamlit, custom UIs) where needed
- Troubleshoot and optimize existing agent flows and AI integrations

### Requirements

- Bachelor’s degree from a recognized Canadian public university  
	*(Degrees from private institutions like TRU or UCW will not be accepted)*
- Strong logic and reasoning ability
- Experience with any of the following:
	- Workflow automation tools (Zapier, Make, Crew.ai, n8n)
		- APIs and no-code/low-code platforms
		- Basic scripting (Python, JS) or agent orchestration
- Comfortable learning business systems like payroll, AP/AR, and CRM with the intent of replacing manual processes
