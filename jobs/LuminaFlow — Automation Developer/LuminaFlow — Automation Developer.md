## Make.com / n8n Automation Developer

![Make.com n8n logo](https://luminaflow.org/cp-images/blogs/automation.webp)

LuminaFlow is a small but experienced web development team focused on performance, automation, and user-centric solutions. We specialize in no-code and low-code tools such as Make.com, Bubble, n8n, and WordPress. We work with small businesses and startups to launch MVPs quickly and streamline internal processes. We’re currently looking for a Make.com and/or n8n automation developer to help us build client workflows and internal automations.

### What you’ll do:

- Build and maintain automations using Make.com and/or n8n
- Integrate third-party services via APIs (Airtable, Notion, Slack, Google Sheets, Telegram, etc.)
- Design workflows with branching logic, loops, and conditions
- Work with webhooks, REST APIs, and JSON
- Document your scenarios and hand them off for support
- Collaborate with our project manager and participate in planning

### What We’re Looking For:

- 6+ months of experience with Make.com and/or n8n
- Strong understanding of automation logic and business process design
- Basic JavaScript skills (for scripting inside modules) — a plus
- Confident working with REST APIs, webhooks, and JSON
- Clean and structured workflow organization
- Self-driven, detail-oriented, and responsible
- English: intermediate (at least comfortable reading documentation)

### Nice to Have:

- Experience with other no-code tools (Zapier, Bubble, Airtable)
- Background in working with agencies or product teams
- UI/UX awareness or understanding of client-side needs
- Experience communicating with clients or stakeholders

### What We Offer:

- 100% remote work with a flexible schedule
- Lean team, minimal bureaucracy
- Direct access to experienced developers and fast feedback
- Growth opportunities within no-code and beyond
- Access to internal training and resources
- Hourly or full time pay (negotiable)

If you’re excited about automation, want to see real impact from your work, and enjoy working in a small but professional team — **we’d love to hear from you!**
